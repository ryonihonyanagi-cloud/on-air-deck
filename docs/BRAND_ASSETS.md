## Product name

Use `ON AIR Deck` in prose and `ON AIR DECK` in large display treatments.

Do not shorten the name to `OnAir`, `On Air`, or `OAD` in public-facing copy.

## Core colors

- Charcoal black for the studio surface.
- Warm ivory for primary labels.
- Copper or amber for BGM and premium accents.
- Broadcast red for live and record states.
- Restrained teal for signal and monitoring states.
