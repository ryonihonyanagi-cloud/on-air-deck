# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free community for contributors and users, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the rights attached to recordings and audio assets.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Prioritize the safety of people using the software during live or recorded sessions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information, recordings, credentials, or correspondence without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or contributor work.
- Submitting audio or code that the contributor does not have the right to distribute.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Until a private maintainer contact is published, report conduct incidents through GitHub’s private repository contact route and do not include sensitive details in public issues.
The public release checklist treats the final private contact channel as a launch blocker.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and consistently as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person is publicly representing the project.
